Privacy

Short, because there is little to say: you listen, and nobody keeps track of you.

Nothing is tracked

  • No analytics, no ads, no accounts and no cookies.
  • Your browser remembers one or two conveniences of its own, like whether the recipe card was open. They stay on your device.
  • While you listen, the server knows your connection is there. It counts how many places are listening, which the front page shows, and forgets you when you leave. Nothing about you is written down.
  • The page fetches its sounds from GitHub, and the site reaches you through Cloudflare; both see the connection, as they do for any site.

No AI makes the music

Meander's music is written in advance as code, and played in your browser by Strudel. The weather turns it through fixed rules you can read in the source. Eversimmer has a live coder, a language model that can write new parts, but on Meander it is switched off.
