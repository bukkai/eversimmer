Credits

Eversimmer FM plays music that never stops. These are the people and projects whose work it plays with.

The software

  • Eversimmer FM is free software under the GNU AGPL 3.0. Its source, at the very version playing now, is on GitHub.
  • The music is played by Strudel, by Felix Roos and the Strudel contributors, also under the AGPL 3.0.

The sounds

  • Grand piano: the Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, under CC BY 3.0, by way of Strudel's dough-samples.
  • Instruments: General MIDI sounds from webaudiofontdata, from Sergey Surikov's WebAudioFont, under the MIT licence.
  • Drums and noises: Dirt-Samples by the TidalCycles community, and drum machines from tidal-drum-machines.
  • Everything else, the synths, pads and basses, is made as it plays.

The weather

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, under CC BY 4.0, from the national weather services whose models it serves.
